Tempo Breach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Short version: Tempo Breach does not collect, sell or share personal information.

What the game stores

Only your game progress: levels cleared, stars, coins, unlocked items, settings, daily missions and best scores. It contains no name, email address, location or contact details.

Where it is stored

On your own device, in your browser's local storage. When you play on CrazyGames, progress is saved through the CrazyGames data service so it can follow your CrazyGames account, if you have one.

What the game does not do

No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, no third-party advertising cookies, no purchases, no chat and no account system of its own. The game never asks for personal information.

The platform

The website that hosts the game (for example CrazyGames) runs its own services and has its own privacy policy, which covers anything the platform itself collects. The game loads the CrazyGames SDK to report loading and gameplay events and to save progress.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all locally saved progress. Progress saved to a CrazyGames account can be managed from that account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear on this page and inside the game (Settings, Privacy Policy). Questions can be sent through the developer's page on the platform where you are playing.
